# Bundled typefaces

These woff2 files are subset-free latin builds fetched from Google Fonts and are
served locally over `file://` so poster rendering stays deterministic and works
offline. No webfont CDN is contacted at render time.

Every family below is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, which
permits bundling and redistribution provided the fonts are not sold on their own
and the licence travels with them.
