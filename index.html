FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

How much ad budget do I need?

We'll recommend a budget for your city and goals in the free review. Many local campaigns start small, around $10 to $15 a day. Ad spend goes straight from your card to Meta.

Do I need to know marketing?

No. ADvantage picks the strategy, makes the content and runs the campaigns. You approve what goes out and get back to coaching.

Which apps does it connect to?

Meta Ads, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, SMS, email, Google Business Profile, Google Drive, your CRM or a spreadsheet, and your booking calendar.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Win-back is a one-time project, and the monthly packages run month to month.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within a week of the review, once we have partner access to your ad account and a folder of your photos.

Will this work with my CRM?

If your CRM has an export or an API, yes. No CRM? A spreadsheet of your families works too.

What if it doesn't work?

If a Fill campaign produces no trial bookings in its first month, your second month is free.

Do you only work with cricket academies?

No. We started with cricket, and we work with youth academies of every kind: swim, dance, chess, abacus, martial arts, badminton, music and tutoring.
